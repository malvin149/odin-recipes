World's Best Lasagna

Recipe adapted from: John Chandler (Allrecipes)

Description

Bringing a global favorite to the digital table, this site offers a step-by-step masterclass in creating Allrecipes' most celebrated lasagna. Visitors can explore the delicate balance of tge rich, slow-simmered meat sauce and the creamy three-cheese blend that has made this recipe a viral sensation.

Ingredients

  • 1 Pound sweet Italian sausage
  • ¾ pound lean ground beef
  • ½ cup minced onion
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 (28 ounce) can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 (6.5 ounce) cans canned tomato sauce
  • 2 (6 ounce) cans tomato paste
  • ½ cup water
  • 2 tablespoons white sugar
  • 4 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley, divided
  • 1 ½ teaspoons dried basil leaves
  • 1 ½ teaspoons salt, divided, or to taste
  • 1 teaspoon italian seasoning
  • ½ teaspoon fennel seeds
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 16 ounces ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • ¾ pound mozzarella cheese, sliced
  • ¾ cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Prepare all the ingredients
  2. Cook sausage, ground beef, onion and garlic in an saucepan over medium heat until browned
  3. Add in crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, tomato paste, and water and stir. Add sugar, 2 tablespoons parsley, basil, 1 teaspoon salt, Italian seasoning, fennel seeds, and pepper for seasoning. Cover and simmer for about 1 ½ hours, stirring occasionally.
  4. Bring a big pot of lightly salted water to boil. Cook lasagna noodles in boiling water for 8 to 10 minutes. Drain noodles with a strainer, and rinse with cold water.
  5. In a mixing bowl, add ricotta cheese with egg, remaining 2 tablespoons parsley, and ½ teaspoon salt, and mix thoroughly.
  6. Preheat the oven to 375 °F (190 ° C)
  7. To put together, spread 1 ½ cups of meat sauce in the bottom of a 9×13-inch baking dish. Arrange 6 noodles over meat sauce, with a little overlapping. Spread with ½ of the ricotta cheese mixture. Top it with ⅓ of the mozzarella cheese slices. Spoon 1 ½ cups meat sauce over mozzarella, and sprinkle with ¼ cup Parmesan cheese.
  8. Layer repeatedly, and top with the remaining mozzarella and Parmesan cheese. Cover with thin foil: to prevent sticking, spray foil with cooking spray or ensure the foil does not touch the cheese.
  9. Bake in the preheated oven for 25 minutes. Take off the foil and bake for an additional 25 minutes.
  10. Rest lasagna for 15 minutes before serving along with preferred juice.
