Italian Spaghetti and Meatballs

Recipe adapted from: Allrecipes

Description

An italian pasta sauce with self-made meatballs simmered over low heat for an intense tomato flavour. It's simple to make the spaghetti sauce ahead of time; add meatballs 30 minutes after reheating and serve with your favorite spaghetti.

Ingredients

Meatballs:
  • ½ pound lean ground beef
  • ½ cup of fresh bread crumbs
  • ½ tablespoon dried parsley
  • ½ tablespoon grated Parmesan cheese
  • ⅛ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • ⅙ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ½ egg, beaten
Sauce:
  • ⅜ cup chopped onion
  • 2 ½ cloves garlic, minced
  • ⅛ cup olive oil
  • 1 (28 ounce) cans whole peeled tomatoes
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon white sugar
  • ½ bay leaf
  • ½ (6 ounce) can tomato paste
  • ⅜ teaspoon dried basil
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper

Instructions for preparation

Steps:
  1. Prepare all the ingredients
  2. Add ground beef, bread crumbs, parsley, ¼ teaspoon black pepper, garlic powder and beaten egg in a big bowl. Stir well and form 12 meatballs. Cover and store in refrigerator until needed.
  3. Saute onion and garlic in olive oil in a big saucepan over medium heat, until onion is half-fried.
  4. Add tomatoes, salt and bay leaf and stir. Cover and let it simmer for 90 minutes under low heat.
  5. Add tomato paste, basil, ½ teaspoon pepper and meatballs and stir. Let it simmer for another 30 or more minutes.
  6. Serve your plated Italian spaghetti sauce with meatballs with grated Parmesan cheese and enjoy!
